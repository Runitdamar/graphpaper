Terms

The simple rules for using GraphPaper.

Using GraphPaper

GraphPaper is free to use. It gathers links to official notices, books and syllabus pages so they are easier to find.

Not an official website

GraphPaper is not run by CBSE, NCERT or any other board. Notices and dates can change, so always confirm them at the official source before you act on them.

Other people's files

GraphPaper does not host board or NCERT files. Links open the official sites, which have their own terms.

No promises

We try to keep things correct and current, but we cannot promise that nothing is missing or out of date. If you spot a mistake, please tell us from the Contact page.

Changes

We may update these terms as GraphPaper grows. The latest version is always on this page.
