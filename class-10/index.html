Find what you need for Class 10

Class 10 ends with CBSE’s first board exam. In most subjects the final marks combine a board paper with internal assessment from your school. Everything for it is here in one place: the official 2026-27 syllabus, sample papers with marking schemes, previous years’ board papers, every NCERT book and the latest CBSE notices that mention Class 10.
