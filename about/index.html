About GraphPaper

Every school notice, in one calm place.

Our idea

Students should not have to hunt through several websites for a circular, date sheet or result. GraphPaper gathers the official CBSE notices, sorts them, and links to the original so you can read the real thing.

What you will find here

Notices: CBSE circulars, date sheets, syllabus changes and results.
Books: NCERT chapter PDFs for Class 1 to 12, linked from ncert.nic.in (English-medium books for now).
Syllabus: the official 2026-27 syllabus for Physics, Chemistry, Maths and Biology.
Notes: coming later.

Not an official website

GraphPaper is not run by any board. Notices can change, so always check them at the official source before you act on them.
