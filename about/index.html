About GraphPaper

Books, syllabus and notices for your class, in one calm place.

Our idea

Students and parents should not have to hunt through several websites for a book, a syllabus or a circular. GraphPaper gathers the official material, sorts it by class, and links to the original so you read the real thing.

What you will find here

NCERT books: every chapter PDF for Class 1 to 12, English-medium books plus the Hindi and Sanskrit language books, with chapter titles, a whole-book download and a printable book list.
Syllabus: the official 2026-27 CBSE syllabus for Class 9 to 12.
Papers and question bank: CBSE sample papers and past board papers for Class 10 and 12, and practice questions from Class 6.
Updates: CBSE circulars, date sheets and results, plus a quick status check for Class 10 and 12.
For parents: plain answers about school stages, exams and report cards, for Classes 1 to 8 and beyond.
Notes: coming later.

How it works

Pick your class once, and your stream for Class 11 and 12. It is remembered on your device, so there is no account to make. Every link goes to the official CBSE or NCERT website. GraphPaper does not host the files.

Not an official website

GraphPaper is not run by any board. Notices can change, so always check them at the official source before you act on them.
