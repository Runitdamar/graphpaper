Privacy

Short version: GraphPaper does not ask for your name, email or phone number.

What stays on your device

There are no accounts and no forms that collect your details. The class you pick is saved in your own browser so the site can remember it. It is never sent to us.

What other services see

The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Links to cbse.gov.in, ncert.nic.in and cbseacademic.nic.in leave this site, and those sites have their own privacy practices.

Your choices

To forget your class choice, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
